{\bfseries exercise notes (quick setup cues)}

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.2em]
  \item \textbf{tibialis raises:} lean back against a wall, heels planted. lift toes toward shins slowly. no rocking.
  \item \textbf{calf raises (straight leg):} stand tall, knees straight. rise onto toes, control the descent.
  \item \textbf{calf raises (bent knee):} slight knee bend, torso upright. targets the deep soleus muscle.
  \item \textbf{ankle mobility (knee to wall):} foot flat, knee tracks forward over toes. heel never lifts.
  \item \textbf{short foot:} gently lift the arch by drawing ball of foot toward heel. do not curl toes.
  \item \textbf{deep nasal breathing:} slow nasal inhale, longer relaxed exhale. diaphragm does the work.
\end{itemize}
